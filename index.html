How the score works

Each lookup runs a live search and produces a composite score from 0-100, built from three weighted categories. This isn't an audited third-party rating — it's compiled on demand from public certifications and recent coverage, so treat it as a starting point rather than the final word.

  • Environmental (40%) — emissions commitments, energy sourcing, environmental controversies
  • Labour & Social (35%) — labour practices, supply chain, human rights coverage
  • Governance (25%) — certifications, transparency, anti-corruption record

Each lookup triggers a live web search checking B Corp certification status, Ethisphere's World's Most Ethical Companies recognition, and recent news or NGO coverage of labour, environmental, and governance issues.

Category scores are estimated from that evidence, then combined using the weights above. Where evidence is thin, the tool notes that explicitly rather than assuming a high score.

Not an audited rating

This is a live, AI-generated estimate for personal decision-making — not a substitute for professional ESG research, and not affiliated with MSCI, Sustainalytics, or similar rating providers.
